Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

Zād Journey – Prayer does not require an account and does not contain advertising or third-party analytics.

Location

If you grant permission, the app uses your precise location to determine prayer times, Qibla direction, and nearby place names. Location access can be disabled in iOS Settings. When calculated prayer times are selected, latitude and longitude are sent securely to the AlAdhan prayer-times service to retrieve a schedule. We do not use location for advertising or tracking.

Data stored on your devices

Locations, settings, cached prayer schedules, and imported schedules are stored locally on your devices and in Apple app-group storage so the app, widgets, and paired Apple Watch can work together.

Third-party services

The app uses Apple location, mapping, widget, and watch connectivity technologies. Calculated schedules use the AlAdhan API. Those services may process technical information under their own privacy terms.

Tracking and sale of data

We do not track you across apps or websites, sell personal information, or use your data for targeted advertising.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the public support page. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.
