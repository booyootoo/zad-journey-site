Zād Journey Support

Need help with prayer times, locations, imported schedules, Qibla, widgets, or the Apple Watch app?

Before reporting a problem

  • Open the iPhone app and confirm that the intended location is selected.
  • Check location permission in iOS Settings if current location or Qibla is unavailable.
  • Open the Apple Watch app after opening the iPhone app to refresh synced prayer data.

Contact support

Create a support request. This is a public issue tracker, so do not include your phone number, precise location, imported timetable, or other sensitive information.
